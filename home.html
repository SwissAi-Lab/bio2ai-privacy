Bio2AI Transcoder

Bio2AI Transcoder is a custom GPT designed to help users work with documents, files, structured data and Google Drive.

Google Drive integration

When a user chooses to connect Google Drive, Bio2AI Transcoder requests authorization through Google's OAuth system.

Access is granted by the individual Google user and applies to that user's Google Drive account. Bio2AI Transcoder may use the authorized access to list, search, read, create, upload, copy, rename, move, modify, organize, share and delete files and folders when requested by the user.

Users remain in control of their Google account and may revoke the application's Google access at any time through their Google Account settings.

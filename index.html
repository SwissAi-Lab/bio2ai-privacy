Privacy Policy for Bio2AI Transcoder

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Bio2AI Transcoder is a custom GPT intended for personal use. It uses Google OAuth to allow an authorized user to access Google Drive through Google APIs.

Google Drive Access

When a user authorizes Google Drive access, Bio2AI Transcoder may perform Google Drive operations requested by the user, including listing, reading, creating, updating, copying, exporting, and deleting files.

Access to Google Drive is granted only after the user completes Google's OAuth authorization process.

Use of Google Data

Google Drive data is accessed only as necessary to perform actions explicitly requested by the user.

This integration communicates with Google APIs and does not operate an independent application server for storing copies of Google Drive files.

Authentication

Authentication is performed using Google OAuth. Users may revoke the application's access through their Google Account security settings.

Data Sharing

The developer of Bio2AI Transcoder does not sell Google Drive data or use it for advertising.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality or data-access practices of Bio2AI Transcoder change.
